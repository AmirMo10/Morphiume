# Observed design references

Observed on **2026-10-12** using web retrieval of the exact requested URLs. This records HTML/text observations, not a rendered visual/mobile/accessibility/performance audit. Interactive checkout, transactions and claimed service quality were not measured. No reference images, logo geometry or copy were reused.

| Exact reference | Actually observed | Limit | User problem and Morphiume response |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Aesop fragrance](https://www.aesop.com/fragrance.html) | Fragrance-family links and buttons, product title/key-note groups, size selector text, prices, guide link, education links and support footer. | Retrieval includes offline/JavaScript notices; size interactions and rendered layout not tested. | Reduce catalog uncertainty with real family links, readable facts and a clear guide route. |
| [Aesop finder](https://www.aesop.com/fragrance-fables-finder/) | Three named stages for family, aroma and mood; selectable prompts, Continue/Back, Retake and final Confirm controls are present in HTML. | Actual transitions, scoring and results were not executed. | Use a short reversible guide with explicit catalog attributes and explainable results; do not copy poetic questions or infer psychology. |
