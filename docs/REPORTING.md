# Periodic financial evidence

`GET /api/admin/reports/period?from=YYYY-MM-DD&to=YYYY-MM-DD` accepts inclusive calendar dates in Asia/Tehran, one to366days. Missing dates default to the30days ending at the supplied end date, or today in Tehran. PostgreSQL uses midnight bounds in that time zone, with an exclusive following midnight; storage remains UTC. Only current enabled owner/finance accounts with a live admin grant can read the report. Responses retain the API's private no-store boundary. The original all-time endpoint remains available during UI integration.

Cash received includes every distinct authoritative receipt. Primary purchase receipts, extra receipts and inventory exceptions are separate. Purchase receipts include the order's shipping/tax amounts and are not a claim about accounting profit. A receipt is counted at its recorded creation time. Successful refunds are counted at the immutable `refund-complete:<id>` order event time, with matching order/type; pending, processing and failed refunds are not cash outflows. Historical succeeded refunds lacking that evidence are counted as undated and are not assigned an invented completion date. Period net cash or net primary purchase cash can be negative when older purchases are refunded in a later period. Integer IRR decimal strings are retained through subtraction and signed aggregate تومان formatting.

Activity currently counts recorded checkout creation, each distinct verified receipt and delivered-shipment events within the window. These are period activity counts, not a shared acquisition cohort, unique people or a conversion percentage. The full product-view/search/cart funnel is separate pending work. Inventory/reservations, unfinished exception receipts and pending/dead jobs are current all-time operational state, explicitly outside the period totals. No customer contact, address, search query, payment-provider reference or refund evidence appears in this DTO.

`GET /api/admin/reports/queue?page=1&pageSize=25` pages unfinished extra/inventory-exception receipts and pending/running/dead jobs in the same read-only snapshot, with a100-item cap and explicit totals. Receipt remaining amounts subtract succeeded refunds; pending/processing refund amounts remain a separate reserved-budget field. This read view does not release budget, retry a payment or mark an ambiguous transfer failed. Job output exposes named IDs, fixed categories, status, attempt count and next availability only; unknown types become `domain.other`, and raw payload/dedupe/provider/error details are excluded. Full native operator acceptance is pending.

The date-input helper searches a bounded range of Gregorian calendar days using the same standardized Persian calendar as its `Intl.DateTimeFormat` display. It preserves date-only values and validates the round trip rather than inventing a leap-year rule or treating a selected report day as a UTC timestamp. Primary reference: [Intl.DateTimeFormat documentation](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/JavaScript/Reference/Global_Objects/Intl/DateTimeFormat).
