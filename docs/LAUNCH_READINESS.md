## Acceptance scenarios and remaining breadth

T01–T03 map to catalog/cart/native OTP suites; T04–T06 to current role/MFA/IDOR checks; T07–T09 and T23/T27/T30 to exact integer amounts, PostgreSQL stock/coupon concurrency and actor/payload idempotency; T10–T15/T26 to controlled verify/reconciliation/receipt/refund cases; T16 to outbox lease/dedup and packaged-worker checks; T17–T20 to media/privacy/recovery/RTL checks; T21/T25 to the native product-to-tracking journey; T28/T29 to stale edits and owned state-guarded returns. T22 requires the **same deployed image's actual isolated recovery**, independently of CI.

The older [T01–T30 matrix](qa/ACCEPTANCE_MATRIX.md) preserves useful per-scenario limits and historical failures; its initial two-engine/older-image statements must not be mistaken for the later three-engine redesign CI. Remaining breadth includes every API tamper/authorization permutation, actual CDN behavior, screen readers/physical devices, real PSP/SMS delivery/outages, first real transaction/refund and real Iran networks. These are not silently converted to passing checks by the aggregate CI count.
